I am currently a Postdoctoral Researcher at the Center for Intelligent Infrastructure at Missouri University of Science & Technology. My work focuses on improving bridge inspection capabilities using multimodal perception systems, 3D reconstruction techniques, computer vision, deep learning, and digital twin technologies.
Prior to this role, I worked as a Postdoctoral Fellow in the Electrical and Computer Engineering Department, where I developed computer vision algorithms for UAV navigation in GPS-denied environments. Developed hybrid frameworks combining classical computer vision and deep learning techniques for vision-based calibration and localization.
I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from the University of Nevada, Las Vegas, focusing on hyperspectral imaging and deep learning for defect detection and quality assessment in fruits and vegetables. I also hold an M.S. in Computer Science from Sharif University of Technology, where I specialized in scientific computation and microscopic image analysis.

My areas of interest include 3D Computer Vision, 3D Reconstruction, Neural Rendering, NeRF, Sensor Fusion, Multiple View Geometry, Deep Learning, UAV Perception, Hyperspectral Imaging, and Machine Learning.
