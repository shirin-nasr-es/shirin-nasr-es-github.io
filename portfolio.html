Fine-Detail 3D Bridge Digital Twin (Ongoing)

Objective: Develop a high-fidelity digital twin of a bridge using UAV imagery and photogrammetry.
The goal is to capture fine structural details that can support the detection of defects such as cracks, spalling, and corrosion, while enabling bridge inspection, condition assessment, and long-term monitoring.
